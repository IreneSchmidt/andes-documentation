To better understand the work dynamics of the team involved in the ANDES project, the DISC behavioral assessment model was used, a widely adopted methodology to identify professional profiles based on four main characteristics: Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, and Conformity.

## What is DISC?

DISC is a behavioral mapping tool that classifies profiles into four styles:

- Dominance (D) – Objective individuals who enjoy challenges, quick decisions, and a focus on results.

- Influence (I) – Communicative, enthusiastic professionals who work well in teams.

- Steadiness (S) – Consistent, calm, and collaborative individuals who value harmony and security.

- Conformity (C) – Analytical, detail-oriented profiles focused on rules and quality.

The test was conducted through the platform [Pacto RH](https://www.pactorh.com.br/teste-disc/disc/faca-agora-teste-disc/), which provides a free and reliable DISC profile assessment.
