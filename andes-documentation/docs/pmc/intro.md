The ANDES project emerges as a direct response to the challenges faced in software development, especially regarding documentation. Often overlooked, this stage is carried out without standardization, in a time-consuming and sometimes inefficient manner. With that in mind, ANDES proposes a practical solution: an extension for VSCode that simplifies and standardizes the creation of technical documentation. This document provides an overview of the project, its objectives, scope, team, risks, and timeline.
